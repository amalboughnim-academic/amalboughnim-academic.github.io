<p><span style="display: inline-block; background: #788BB0; color: #333; padding: 0.15rem 0.5rem; border-radius: 0.25rem; font-size: 0.85em; font-weight: 500; margin-right: 0.4rem; vertical-align: middle; text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 0.03em;">New Position</span> I'm delighted to announce that I've secured a 1-year postdoctoral research contract at the Bordeaux School of Economics, where I'll be working on an interdisciplinary project on research integrity in biomedical research, part of the <a href="https://restores.univ-rennes.fr/" target="_blank">ANR RestoRES</a> project, led by <a href="https://restores.univ-rennes.fr/interlocutors/florian-naudet" target="_blank">Dr. Florian Naudet</a>. The project brings together a multidisciplinary team — economists, physicians, methodologists, biostatisticians, and data engineers — spanning France, Canada, and Ireland, across several workpackages.</p>

<p>Within this project, I'll be working closely with my workpackage leader, <a href="http://ncarayol.u-bordeaux.fr/" target="_blank">Pr. Nicolas Carayol</a>, on conflict-of-interest disclosure in biomedical publications, while also engaging regularly with the broader project team. Our aim is threefold: to map the current state of disclosure practices, identify what determines whether ties are disclosed, and assess the possible impact of conflict-of-interest ties on researchers' scientific output.</p>
