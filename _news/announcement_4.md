## Workshop on the Economics of Science and Innovation — thank you

Thank you to everyone who made our Workshop on the Economics of Science and
Innovation (EoSI) at [Bordeaux Sciences Economiques](https://www.bse.u-bordeaux.fr/) such a success.

We are especially grateful to our keynote speakers, [Ina Ganguli](https://scholar.harvard.edu/iganguli/home) (University of Massachusetts Amherst) and [Sotaro Shibayama](https://sotaroshibayama.weebly.com/) (Institute for Future Initiatives, University of Tokyo), for their inspiring talks, and to all the participants who traveled to present their work.

We would also like to thank the members of the local scientific committee for their time and thoughtful engagement throughout the workshop.

This event was made possible thanks to the support of the [Young Scholars Initiative](https://www.ineteconomics.org/ysi), Diego Useche, and the Département de recherche ECOr.

Wishing everyone a great summer ahead!
