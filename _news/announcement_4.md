Our Workshop on the Economics of Science and Innovation (EoSI) at [Bordeaux Sciences Economiques](https://www.bse.u-bordeaux.fr/) has concluded!

Many thanks to our keynote speakers, [Ina Ganguli](https://scholar.harvard.edu/iganguli/home) (UMass Amherst) and [Sotaro Shibayama](https://sotaroshibayama.weebly.com/) (University of Tokyo), and to all participants and the local scientific committee.

This event was made possible thanks to the support of the [Young Scholars Initiative](https://www.ineteconomics.org/ysi) (with the assistance of [Daniel De Sousa](https://danielf-souza.github.io/)), Project-ANR-23-CE26-0003 (thanks to [Diego Useche](https://www.bse.u-bordeaux.fr/membres/diego-useche/)), and the Département de recherche ECOr.

Wishing everyone a great summer ahead!
